1. Client-Side Media Processing (Zero Server Uploads)

Unlike traditional online editors or cloud-based AI inpainting tools, Gemini Watermark Remover runs entirely inside your client browser. When you select, drag, or drop an image (PNG, JPG, WebP) or video (MP4, WebM, MOV) into the application:

  • No Server Transmission: Your media files are loaded directly into your browser's local memory via the HTML5 File and Blob APIs. They are never uploaded to our servers, third-party clouds, or external storage buckets.
  • Local GPU Acceleration: Frame decoding, mathematical alpha-channel calculations, and video re-encoding are executed directly on your device utilizing HTML5 Canvas, OffscreenCanvas, and WebCodecs hardware acceleration.
  • Immediate Discard: When you refresh the webpage, switch tabs, or close your browser, all processed frames and temporary memory buffers are instantly purged by your device's garbage collector.
Zero Knowledge Architecture: We have neither the technical capability nor the desire to view, store, monitor, or analyze your media files. Your personal photographs, artistic creations, and confidential assets remain strictly on your machine.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require you to register an account, log in, or provide any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) to use Gemini Watermark Remover. The only data processed is:

  • Log Data & Server Metrics: When accessing our static site hosted on GitHub Pages (or our CDN providers), standard web server access logs are recorded automatically (such as internet protocol (IP) address, browser user-agent, operating system, referring/exit URLs, date/time stamps, and ISP) for technical diagnostic, abuse prevention, and infrastructure reliability purposes.
  • Local Browser Preferences: We use HTML5 sessionStorage solely to remember which panel tab you have selected (Image Remover vs. Video Remover) for the duration of your browser session. This data never leaves your device.
  • Voluntary Communications: If you reach out to us via email we receive the email address, username, and message content you deliberately submit so we can assist you.

3. Cookies & Web Beacons

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device to store preferences or track website interactions.

Gemini Watermark Remover does not use proprietary tracking cookies for its core watermark removal functionality. However, third-party services integrated on the site (such as analytics providers, font CDNs, and advertising networks) may place cookies on your web browser.

You can choose to disable or selectively turn off our cookies or third-party cookies in your browser settings, or by managing preferences in your Internet Security software. Please note that disabling cookies does not impact the local watermark removal tool.

5. GDPR Compliance (European Economic Area Rights)

For visitors residing within the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, your data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) include:

  • The Right to Access: You have the right to request copies of any personal data we hold about you.
  • The Right to Rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You have the right to request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.
  • The Right to Restrict Processing: You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data under certain conditions.
  • The Right to Object to Processing: You have the right to object to our processing of your personal data.
  • The Right to Data Portability: You have the right to request the transfer of your data to another organization.

Because we do not collect personal identities, account profiles, or media files, we generally hold zero personal data about individual users. If you submit a request, we will respond within one month.

Protecting the privacy of young children is especially important to us. Gemini Watermark Remover is not structured or directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under 13.

8. Data Retention & Security

Since all video frame extraction and image alpha unblending occur locally inside your client sandbox, your data is inherently protected from intercepted transmissions and unauthorized server access.
