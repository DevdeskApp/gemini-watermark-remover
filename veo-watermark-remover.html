The Veo and Google Flow Watermark

Videos made with Veo 3, in the Gemini app or in Google Flow, can carry a small visible mark near the bottom right corner on top of the invisible SynthID watermark. Like the image sparkle, the visible mark is blended over the frame at partial opacity, so the footage underneath is still recoverable.

The video remover reverses that blend on every frame, then rebuilds the clip with the original audio.

How to Clean a Veo 3 Clip

  1. Open the Video Remover tab and drop your clip (MP4, WebM or MOV).
  2. Pick a preset: "Veo (Corner)" for classic Veo exports, or "Gemini Omni & Flow (Adaptive)" for clips from Flow and the Gemini app.
  3. Check the zoomed previews on the first frame and nudge Strength, Size Scale or Position if an outline shows.
  4. Click Remove & Export Video MP4 and keep the tab open until the progress bar finishes.

Mark Position for Common Video Sizes

The Veo preset sizes the mark at about one fifteenth of the shorter side, with a gap of one tenth of the shorter side from the right and bottom edges. For typical Veo resolutions that works out to:

What Happens to Your Video

  • Decoding and encoding use WebCodecs, the video engine built into your browser, so the clip never leaves your device.
  • The output is an H.264 MP4 at the original resolution and frame rate, encoded at a high bitrate. As with any re-encode, it is not bit-for-bit identical to the source, but the difference is hard to see.
  • The main audio track is carried over, so dialogue, music and effects stay in sync.
  • Browser support: WebCodecs is needed. Recent Chrome and Edge on desktop work best; if your browser lacks it, the page tells you instead of failing silently.

Tips for the Cleanest Result

  • Use the original download from Flow or Gemini, not a screen recording or a clip re-shared through a messaging app.
  • If the clip was cropped to a new aspect ratio, the mark moves; line it up with Position X and Y before exporting.
  • Long 4K clips take longer and need more memory. Close other heavy tabs while exporting.
  • Trim and edit after cleaning, so the editor works from the clean frames.

Use It Responsibly

Removing the visible logo changes how an image or clip looks. It does not change what it is. The file still carries Google's invisible SynthID watermark, and many platforms ask you to label AI generated media. Clean the corner logo for layouts, thumbnails and presentations, keep the AI label wherever your platform or client expects one, and follow Google's terms for the product you generated the media with. Never use a cleaned file to pass AI content off as a real photo or recording.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I remove the Veo 3 watermark without blurring the video?

Yes. The tool reverses the transparent blend of the mark on each frame instead of blurring or inpainting the corner, so the detail underneath comes back.

Does the cleaned Veo video keep its sound?

Yes. The main audio track from your clip is copied into the new MP4, so speech and music stay in sync.

What format is the exported video?

An H.264 MP4 at the original resolution and frame rate. MP4, WebM and MOV files are accepted as input.

Does it remove SynthID from Veo videos?

No. SynthID is invisible and stays in the video. Only the visible corner mark is removed.

Why does the export stop or fail?

Video export needs WebCodecs. Use a recent Chrome or Edge, keep the tab in the foreground, and try a shorter or lower resolution clip if memory runs out.
