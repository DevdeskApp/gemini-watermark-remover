What the Nano Banana Watermark Is

Nano Banana is the popular nickname for the Gemini image model that creates and edits pictures right inside the Gemini app. Every picture it saves gets a small, semi transparent four point sparkle in the bottom right corner. The sparkle is blended on top of the picture at partial opacity, which is good news: the original colours are still there underneath, just mixed with white.

Because the mark is a known shape blended in a known way, it can be reversed with arithmetic instead of guessed with AI. That is exactly what this remover does.

Remove the Sparkle in Four Steps

  1. Open the remover and stay on the Image Remover tab.
  2. Drop your Gemini image (PNG, JPG or WebP). Nothing is uploaded: the file is read by your browser.
  3. Keep the preset on "Gemini / Nano Banana (Adaptive)". It sizes and places the mask from the image dimensions. Check the two zoomed previews; the cleaned side should show no outline.
  4. Click Remove & Export Image. The result downloads as a PNG at the original resolution.

Where the Sparkle Sits at Common Sizes

The adaptive preset scales the mask with the shorter side of the image. At 1536 pixels it looks for a 96 pixel sparkle sitting 192 pixels in from the right and bottom edges, and everything shrinks in proportion on smaller images. These are the positions it uses for common Gemini output sizes, handy if you need to line up a cropped or resized image by hand with the Position X and Y sliders:

Older Gemini images often place the sparkle much closer to the corner (a 64 pixel gap at 1536 pixels). If the mask misses, switch to Classic Corner (Fixed); the tool also tries to detect which layout your image uses.

Fixing a Faint Outline or Halo

  • A light ghost of the sparkle remains: raise Strength (Gain) a little from 0.60.
  • A dark patch appears instead: the strength is too high; lower it until the patch disappears.
  • The outline is offset to one side: the image was probably cropped or resized after download. Move Position X / Y a few pixels at a time while watching the zoomed preview.
  • The mark looks larger or smaller than the mask: adjust Size Scale.
  • Only "Classic Corner (Fixed)" lines up: the image uses the older layout with the sparkle close to the corner; keep that preset.

Start from the original download when you can. Screenshots and images re-saved as JPG lose a little colour detail, so the arithmetic has slightly less to work with and a faint edge can remain.

Why Not Just Use an AI Eraser?

Generic object erasers paint new pixels over the corner. On skies and flat walls that looks fine, but on hair, text, patterns or faces it smears detail and can invent things that were never there. Unblending does not invent anything: it solves the blending equation for each pixel and gives back the colour that was underneath.

Use It Responsibly

Removing the visible logo changes how an image or clip looks. It does not change what it is. The file still carries Google's invisible SynthID watermark, and many platforms ask you to label AI generated media. Clean the corner logo for layouts, thumbnails and presentations, keep the AI label wherever your platform or client expects one, and follow Google's terms for the product you generated the media with. Never use a cleaned file to pass AI content off as a real photo or recording.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does removing the Nano Banana sparkle remove SynthID too?

No. SynthID is an invisible watermark spread through the image pixels. This tool only removes the visible sparkle, so AI detectors that read SynthID can still identify the image.

Will the cleaned image lose quality?

The export is a PNG at the original resolution, so nothing is compressed away. Inside the sparkle area the colours are recovered by calculation; on a clean original download the result is very close to what was underneath.

Can I remove the watermark from a Gemini image on my phone?

Yes. The image remover runs in mobile browsers such as Chrome and Safari. Larger images take a moment longer on older phones.

Why is there still a faint outline after cleaning?

Usually the image was resized, cropped or re-saved as JPG after download. Fine tune Strength, Size Scale and Position in the live tuner and watch the zoomed preview until the outline disappears.

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No. The file is processed on your device with the browser's canvas. It never leaves your computer or phone.
