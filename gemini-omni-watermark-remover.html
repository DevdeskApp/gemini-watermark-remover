About Gemini Omni Videos

Gemini Omni is Google's multimodal video model, announced at Google I/O 2026. It turns any mix of text, images, audio and video into a short clip, and you can edit the result by chatting with it. It powers video creation in the Gemini app and is also available in Google Flow and Google Vids.

Clips saved from the Gemini app carry the same four point sparkle used on Gemini images, blended into the bottom right corner of every frame. This page shows how to remove that visible sparkle cleanly.

Open the Video Remover

Remove the Sparkle From an Omni Clip

  1. Download the clip from the Gemini app (or Flow) in its original quality.
  2. Open the Video Remover tab and drop the file in.
  3. Choose "Gemini Omni & Flow (Adaptive)". It sizes the sparkle mask from the shorter side of the video and sets it a little further in from the corner than the classic Veo preset.
  4. Compare the zoomed previews, adjust if needed, then click Remove & Export Video MP4.

Where the Adaptive Preset Looks

The adaptive preset makes the mask about one fifteenth of the shorter side and places it one tenth of the shorter side in from the corner, plus an extra 24 pixels at 720p that grows with the resolution. For the sizes Omni clips usually come in:

If a clip was cropped for Shorts or Reels after download, the sparkle shifts with the crop. Use Position X and Y to move the mask onto it.

Chat Edits and Re-Downloads

Because Omni lets you refine a video by chatting, you may end up with several versions of the same scene. Clean only the final version you plan to use: every export is a fresh re-encode, so cleaning once from the original download keeps the most detail. If you cut several Omni clips together, clean each clip first and edit afterwards.

If the Sparkle Does Not Fully Disappear

  • Faint white outline: raise Strength (Gain) slightly.
  • Dark smudge: lower Strength (Gain).
  • Mask and sparkle do not overlap: fine tune Position X / Y or Size Scale, or try the "Standard Sparkle" preset.
  • Heavily compressed clip: start from the original download rather than a copy shared through social apps.

Use It Responsibly

Removing the visible logo changes how an image or clip looks. It does not change what it is. The file still carries Google's invisible SynthID watermark, and many platforms ask you to label AI generated media. Clean the corner logo for layouts, thumbnails and presentations, keep the AI label wherever your platform or client expects one, and follow Google's terms for the product you generated the media with. Never use a cleaned file to pass AI content off as a real photo or recording.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Gemini Omni?

Gemini Omni is Google's multimodal video model, announced at Google I/O 2026. It creates short videos from any mix of text, images, audio and video, and it is used for video creation in the Gemini app, Google Flow and Google Vids.

Can I remove the Gemini sparkle from Omni videos for free?

Yes. The video remover is free, needs no account and processes the clip on your own device.

Which preset should I use for Gemini Omni clips?

Start with "Gemini Omni & Flow (Adaptive)". If the mask does not line up, try "Standard Sparkle" and fine tune the position sliders.

Is the audio from my Omni video kept?

Yes. The main audio track is copied into the exported MP4.

Does cleaning the video remove SynthID?

No. SynthID is invisible and remains in the video, so the clip can still be identified as AI generated.
