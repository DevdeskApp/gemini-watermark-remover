Quick Support FAQ

• Why didn't a video download complete?

Video processing uses hardware WebCodecs. On very large 4K files, older mobile devices may run out of memory. Try closing background tabs or processing on a desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, or Brave recommended).

• How do I report a DMCA copyright concern?

Please send full details including the URL, identification of the copyrighted work, and authorization proof to support@devdeskapp.com. See our Disclaimer & DMCA Policy for complete instructions.

• Can I request support for a new AI model?

Yes! If Google releases new Gemini or Veo variations with different watermark positions or opacities, message our support team at support@devdeskapp.com with sample dimensions so we can tune the preset matrix.
